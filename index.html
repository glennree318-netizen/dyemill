About DyeMill

DyeMill is a free color palette generator for designers and developers. Pick a base color, choose a harmony, lock what you love and re-roll the rest with the spacebar. Check WCAG contrast, extract palettes from photos, export to CSS or Tailwind - no account, no tracking, works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to save palettes?

No. Everything lives in your browser (localStorage) - locked colors, recent palettes, no login, nothing uploaded.

What do the contrast grades mean?

WCAG ratios: 4.5:1+ passes AA for normal text, 3:1+ for large text, 7:1+ passes AAA. DyeMill computes them live from any two palette colors.

How does image extraction work?

Your photo is downscaled in-browser, pixels are bucketed by color, and the five most distinct dominant colors become your palette. The photo never leaves your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. DyeMill is an installable PWA - after your first visit it keeps working without internet.
